Services

What you can bring it in for

Ordinary workshop work done with dealer equipment: from an oil change to an electrical fault other garages could not solve.

  • Mechanical repairs

    Engine, brakes, suspension and transmission faults. Cars other garages refused have arrived here and left fixed.

  • Servicing and oil changes

    Scheduled maintenance at the manufacturer's intervals. It is the workshop's most common job and the one with the shortest wait.

  • Air conditioning and heating

    Regassing, finding leaks and fixing a blower or heater that stops working all at once — in summer too.

  • Diagnosis of tricky faults

    When the car does something odd and nobody can find why, we spend the time it takes rather than swapping parts blind.

  • Original parts

    We fit original parts. In one documented case the part arrived within 24 hours and the car was ready in 48.

  • Warranty work and a clear invoice

    We are a dealer workshop, so warranty repairs are handled here and the invoice itemises labour and parts.

If what you need is not listed here, call and we'll look at it: a two-minute call beats a wasted trip.

Why us

A large workshop that still answers like a local garage

Tallers Garcia Vilanova sits on Ronda d'Europa, just off the entrance to Vilanova. It is a dealer workshop, with the parts logistics and staff that implies, but what people keep praising in the reviews is the reception desk that picks up the phone and tells you straight.

The reviews repeat the same story: someone turned up without an appointment, on holiday, with a car full of problems, and drove away fixed within a day or two. They also name Bibiana on reception, who is the person you reach when you call.

We are not the cheapest workshop and we do not pretend to be: as a dealer we work at official rates. What we do is tell you the cost before we start, and not invent work the car does not need.

  • Original parts with direct factory logistics

Book a slot

How it works, in three steps

There is no booking form: the phone is faster and lets us ask what we need before you drive over.

  1. Call and describe the problem

    A noise, a warning light, a service that is due, air that will not cool. That is usually enough for us to say whether it needs to come in.

  2. We give you a day and a time

    We tell you which day to bring it and, for a short job, whether you can wait. If parts are needed, we say when they will arrive before taking anything apart.

  3. Collect it with the bill clear

    When it is ready we go through what was changed and what was not. If something serious turns up mid-job, we call before carrying on.
